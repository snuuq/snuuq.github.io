This chapter introduces the flow-based view, connecting normalizing flows and neural ODEs to the flow matching framework. It explains how to construct probability paths and velocity fields between distributions, and treats generation as following a learned velocity field from noise to data.

Chapter 5 of [The Principles of Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890).
