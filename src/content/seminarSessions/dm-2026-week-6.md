This chapter covers how to steer diffusion models toward desired outputs. It works through classifier guidance, classifier-free guidance, and training-free guidance, and connects model alignment from reinforcement learning to direct preference optimization.

Chapter 8 of [The Principles of Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890).
