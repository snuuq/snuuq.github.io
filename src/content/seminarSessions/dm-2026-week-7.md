This chapter studies advanced numerical solvers that accelerate sampling from diffusion models. It develops DDIM, DEIS, and the DPM-Solver and DPM-Solver++ families, and situates them within the broader landscape of PF-ODE solvers and their numerical analogues.

Chapter 9 of [The Principles of Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890).
